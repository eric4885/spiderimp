Spider Solitaire FAQ

Short answers about free Spider Solitaire on SpiderImp: rules, Daily Challenge, scoring, empty columns, and playing with no download. For illustrated steps, see How to Play.

What is Spider Solitaire?

Spider Solitaire is a classic two-deck patience (solitaire) card game. The goal is to build same-suit sequences from King down to Ace and clear the tableau. Unlike Klondike solitaire, Spider uses ten columns and focuses on suit runs. On SpiderImp you can play free Spider Solitaire online with three difficulty levels: 1 suit (easiest), 2 suits (medium), and 4 suits (hardest, full two-deck challenge).

How do you play Spider Solitaire free online?

On spiderimp.com: choose 1-suit, 2-suit, or 4-suit mode, then drag cards between columns. You may place a card on the next-higher rank (suit does not matter for placement). Only same-suit descending stacks move together as a group. Empty columns accept any card or valid stack. Face-down cards flip face-up when uncovered. When no column is empty, click the Deal pile (top-left) to deal one card onto each of the ten columns. Completed King-to-Ace same-suit runs move automatically to the Completed area. Clear all eight runs to win. Illustrated steps: How to Play.

What is the difference between 1 suit, 2 suits, and 4 suits?

1 suit is the best beginner mode. 2 suits adds more planning for casual daily play. 4 suits uses a full two-deck mix and is the classic hard mode. Compare them on the difficulty page.

Do I need to download Spider Solitaire?

No. SpiderImp is spider solitaire with no download—play instantly in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari on Windows, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, Android tablet, or phone. See play on mobile.

What is the Spider Solitaire Daily Challenge?

Every calendar day, SpiderImp deals the same layout for each difficulty so you can retry and compare. Restart keeps today’s deal; tomorrow brings a new one. Scores stay on your device. Details: Daily Challenge.

How is scoring calculated on SpiderImp?

SpiderImp ranks games by moves first (fewer is better), then by time. There is no subtractive Windows-style point score. Best Scores and Statistics are stored locally for each difficulty. See scoring explained.

Why can’t I deal when a column is empty?

Classic Spider requires every column to have at least one card before you deal. An empty column is a parking spot for rearranging runs. Fill it first, then click Deal. Full answer: empty-column rule.

Spider Solitaire vs Klondike and FreeCell?

Klondike builds foundations Ace-up by suit. FreeCell uses open cells. Spider focuses on ten-column tableau building and same-suit King-to-Ace clears. Comparison: Spider vs Klondike vs FreeCell.

Where are my scores stored? Can I play offline?

Best Scores, Statistics, Daily personal bests, volume, and resume state are stored locally in your browser. Clearing site data for spiderimp.com removes them. You need a network connection to load the site. See Privacy.

Is Spider Solitaire good for kids and seniors?

Yes. One-suit Spider is a friendly introduction for kids learning card ranks, for seniors who enjoy patience games, and for anyone who wants a calm puzzle. Mute sound for quiet play. Undo makes mistakes easy to reverse.
