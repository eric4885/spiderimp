Undo and Hint in Spider Solitaire

SpiderImp includes free Undo and Hint on every deal—no premium unlock. Undo reverses your last move; Hint highlights one legal move when you are stuck.

How does Undo work?

Press Z or tap Undo in the control bar. SpiderImp rewinds one move at a time so you can try a different line— especially useful after a bad deal from the stock.

What does Hint do?

Press H or tap Hint. If a legal move exists, SpiderImp glows a useful card and target column. Hint does not auto-win the deal; it nudges you when scanning ten columns is tiring.

Other controls

  • P or Pause — freeze the timer
  • D / Space — deal when every column has a card
  • Restart — same deal again (Daily keeps today's layout)
