About SpiderImp

SpiderImp is a free browser Spider Solitaire site at spiderimp.com. It is a small project built for quick play, clear rules, and a classic feel—without an app-store download or an account.

What is SpiderImp?

SpiderImp is an English-language Spider Solitaire game you play in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Choose 1, 2, or 4 suits, start a random deal or the Daily Challenge, and clear eight same-suit King-to-Ace runs. There is no signup and no install.

What you get

  • 1 / 2 / 4 suit difficulty modes
  • Daily Challenge with the same deal for everyone each calendar day
  • Undo, Hint, Pause, Restart, and volume control
  • Best Scores and Statistics stored locally on your device (not on a SpiderImp account)
  • Works on desktop and phones, tablets, and Chromebooks

Who it is for

Players who want a calm, classic patience game—at home, on a break, or on the go. The site is written in English and works worldwide wherever you have a modern browser.

How we treat scores and privacy

Wins are ranked by fewest moves, then fastest time. Progress stays in your browser’s local storage. Clearing site data for spiderimp.com removes it. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Who runs the site?

SpiderImp is an independent free game project. For bugs, rules questions, or content-use requests, email support@spiderimp.com or use the contact page. Please include your browser and device when reporting a bug.
