Play Spider Solitaire on mobile

Play Spider Solitaire online free on phone, iPad, or Chromebook—no download or app install. Open spiderimp.com in Safari, Chrome, or Edge and tap Play. There is no App Store or Play Store install.

How do you play on a phone or tablet?

Tap a card, then tap a valid target column—or drag. The same rules apply as desktop: same-suit runs move together, and you cannot deal while a column is empty. Use on-screen Undo, Hint, Restart, Menu, and FAQ.

Does it work on iPad and Chromebook?

Yes. Any modern browser is enough. On Chromebooks, treat it like a desktop session (click-to-move or drag). Add the site to your home screen as a bookmark if you want one-tap access—still not a separate app.

What about sound, pause, and resume?

Mute from the controls for quiet play. Pause (P on a keyboard, or the Pause control) freezes the timer. Unfinished games can be resumed from the main menu as long as you have not cleared site data for spiderimp.com.

Is an account required?

No. Scores stay on the device. Switching phones does not carry Best Scores unless you copied a result yourself. See Privacy.
