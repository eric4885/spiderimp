Spider Solitaire vs Klondike vs FreeCell

Klondike is the common “Windows Solitaire.” FreeCell uses open cells as helpers. Spider Solitaire uses two decks, ten columns, and same-suit King-to-Ace clears—a longer, more strategic patience game.

Spider Klondike FreeCell
Decks Two (104 cards) One (52) One (52)
Layout Ten columns Seven columns + stock Eight columns + four free cells
Win condition Eight same-suit King-to-Ace runs Four Ace-up suit foundations Four Ace-up suit foundations
Feel Long session, empty-column tactics Faster, luck of the draw Nearly all deals solvable with planning

When should you play Spider?

Choose Spider if you want a deeper tableau puzzle than Klondike, with 1-suit for learning and 4-suit for the classic hard mix. SpiderImp adds a shared Daily deal and local move/time scores—no download.

How is Spider different on the table?

You build down by rank on the columns, but you only remove a run when it is the same suit from King to Ace. You may not deal while a column is empty. Those two rules drive most strategy. See Spider rules and strategy.
