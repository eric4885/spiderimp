Easy Spider Solitaire (1 suit)

The easiest Spider Solitaire mode is 1 suit: almost every descending stack moves together. SpiderImp adds undo, hint, and mute for calm, accessible free play—no download.

Why 1 suit for seniors and beginners?

One suit removes most suit-confusion. You can focus on uncovering cards and using empty columns. When ready, step up to 2 suits or read the full difficulty comparison.

Accessibility on SpiderImp

  • Undo (Z) — reverse a mistake
  • Hint (H) — highlight one legal move
  • Mute — quiet play
  • Click-to-move or drag on desktop and tablet

No account, no install

Play free at spiderimp.com with 1 suit selected. Scores stay in your browser—see Privacy.
